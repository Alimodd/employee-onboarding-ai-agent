Overview
--------
Northstar Analytics supports a balanced approach to remote work. This policy
explains how many remote days are allowed, how approval works, and the
expectations employees must meet while working away from the office.

Remote Work Allowance
---------------------
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. The remaining working days are
expected to be on-site unless a specific arrangement has been agreed. Standard
working hours remain Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00, whether an employee is
on-site or remote.

Approval
--------
Manager approval is required for a remote-work arrangement. Managers consider the
needs of the role, team collaboration, and current projects. Approved
arrangements may be reviewed if business needs change.

Availability
------------
Employees must remain reachable during working hours while working remotely. This
means being available on approved communication tools and responding within a
reasonable time. Remote work is not a substitute for leave or reduced hours.

Data Protection
---------------
Confidential company data must not be handled on public or shared devices.
Employees should use company-approved equipment and secure networks. Sensitive
information should never be accessed from unsecured public places where it could
be seen by others.

Role-Specific Requirements
--------------------------
Some roles may require more on-site presence than others. For example, roles that
depend on in-person collaboration or physical equipment may have a lower remote
allowance. Managers will communicate any such requirements clearly.

Summary
-------
- Up to 2 remote-work days per week.
- Manager approval is required.
- Employees must remain reachable during working hours.
- Confidential data must not be handled on public devices.
- Some roles may require more on-site presence.
