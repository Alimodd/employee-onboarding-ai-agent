Overview
--------
This guide summarises the main benefits available to employees at Northstar
Analytics. It is a high-level overview; specific details may be confirmed with HR.

Public Transport Reimbursement
------------------------------
Employees can receive public transport reimbursement according to the fictional
company scheme. The scheme encourages sustainable commuting and helps reduce
travel costs. HR can explain how to submit a claim.

Health Insurance
----------------
The company provides a basic health-insurance contribution. This contributes
towards an employee's health coverage as part of the overall benefits package.
Details of the coverage are available from HR.

Training Budget
---------------
Employees have access to a training budget, subject to manager approval. The
budget supports learning that is relevant to the employee's role and development.
Requests should be discussed with the manager before any commitment is made.

Meal Vouchers
-------------
Meal vouchers are provided on eligible working days. Eligibility depends on the
company scheme and the days actually worked. The vouchers are a common part of the
overall compensation package.

Eligibility
-----------
Benefits may depend on employment type, such as full-time or part-time status.
Some benefits may be adjusted proportionally. Employees can contact HR to confirm
which benefits apply to their specific situation.
