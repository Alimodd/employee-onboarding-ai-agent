Overview
--------
Welcome to Northstar Analytics. This checklist helps new employees complete the
key steps needed to get set up and productive during their first days.

First Steps
-----------
- Complete your employee profile with the required personal and role details.
- Sign the required internal documents so your records are complete.
- Set up your company email account using the instructions provided.

Security Setup
--------------
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your company accounts. This is a
  core security step and aligns with the IT Security Policy.

People
------
- Meet your manager to discuss your role, goals, and early priorities.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, who can answer everyday questions and help you
  settle in.

Policy Review
-------------
- Review the leave, remote-work, security, and conduct policies so you understand
  the main expectations. These are the annual leave, remote work, IT security, and
  code of conduct documents in this knowledge base.

Check-In
--------
- Complete your first-week check-in with your manager to review progress and raise
  any questions.

Summary
-------
- Complete your employee profile.
- Sign required internal documents.
- Set up company email.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Meet your manager and onboarding buddy.
- Review leave, remote-work, security, and conduct policies.
- Complete your first-week check-in.
