Overview
--------
Good security is everyone's responsibility. This policy sets out simple, practical
rules that help protect company systems, data, and people from common threats.

Approved Devices
----------------
Employees must use company-approved devices for work tasks. Approved devices are
configured with the required security controls. Personal devices should not be
used to store or process confidential company information.

Passwords
---------
Do not share passwords with anyone, including colleagues. Each person is
responsible for the activity on their own account. Passwords should be unique and
kept private at all times.

Multi-Factor Authentication
---------------------------
Employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on company accounts that
support it. MFA adds a second layer of protection so that a stolen password alone
is not enough to access an account.

Suspicious Emails
-----------------
Report suspicious emails, such as unexpected attachments or requests for
credentials, through the internal reporting channel. Do not click links or open
attachments you were not expecting. When in doubt, ask before acting.

Data Handling
-------------
Do not upload company documents to unauthorized services or personal cloud
accounts. Company data should only be stored in approved locations. This helps
prevent accidental leaks and keeps information under proper control.

Summary
-------
- Use company-approved devices.
- Do not share passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Report suspicious emails.
- Do not upload company documents to unauthorized services.
