Overview
--------
Northstar Analytics provides annual leave so employees can rest and maintain a
healthy work-life balance. This policy explains the amount of leave available,
how to request it, and how approval works.

Leave Entitlement
-----------------
Every employee receives 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave
is counted in working days and does not include weekends or recognised company
holidays. Employees who join partway through the year receive a proportional
amount for their first year.

Requesting Leave
----------------
Leave requests should normally be submitted at least 10 working days in advance.
Requesting early helps managers plan team coverage and reduces scheduling
conflicts. Requests are made through the standard internal HR process.

Approval
--------
Manager approval is required before any leave is confirmed. Managers consider
team workload, project deadlines, and existing approved leave when reviewing a
request. Employees should not finalise travel or other plans until their leave
has been approved.

Carryover
---------
Unused annual leave is not automatically carried over into the next calendar
year. Employees are encouraged to plan their leave across the year so that their
full entitlement can be used. Any exceptional situations should be discussed with
HR in advance.

Exceptional Cases
-----------------
For situations not covered by this policy, such as extended leave or unusual
circumstances, employees should contact HR directly. HR can explain the options
available and any additional steps required.

Summary
-------
- 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.
- Submit requests at least 10 working days in advance.
- Manager approval is always required.
- Unused leave does not automatically carry over.
- Contact HR for exceptional cases.
