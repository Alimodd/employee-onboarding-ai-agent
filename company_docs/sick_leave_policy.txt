Overview
--------
This policy explains what employees should do when they are unable to work due to
illness. The goal is a clear, respectful process that helps teams plan while
protecting employee privacy.

Reporting an Absence
--------------------
Employees must notify their direct manager before 09:00 on the first day of
absence. Early notice allows the team to adjust work and coverage for the day.
Notification should be made through the normal agreed communication method.

Estimated Duration
------------------
When possible, employees should provide an estimated duration of their absence.
This helps managers plan workloads and reassign urgent tasks. If the situation
changes, employees should update their manager as soon as they can.

Medical Documentation
---------------------
Medical documentation may be requested according to the internal procedure. The
requirement depends on the length and nature of the absence. HR can explain when
documentation is needed and how it should be provided.

Privacy
-------
Health details should not be shared in unnecessary detail. Employees only need to
report that they are unwell and, where relevant, the expected duration. Managers
and colleagues should treat all such information with discretion.

Getting Help
------------
If an employee is unsure how to report an absence or has questions about the
process, HR can clarify the reporting steps. HR is the point of contact for any
exceptional or unclear situations.

Summary
-------
- Notify your direct manager before 09:00 on the first day.
- Provide an estimated duration when possible.
- Medical documentation may be requested per internal procedure.
- Do not share unnecessary health details.
- Contact HR to clarify the reporting process.
