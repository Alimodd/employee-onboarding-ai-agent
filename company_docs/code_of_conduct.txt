Overview
--------
The Code of Conduct describes how everyone at Northstar Analytics is expected to
behave. It exists to keep the workplace safe, respectful, and trustworthy for all
employees.

Respect
-------
Treat colleagues respectfully at all times. Listen to others, communicate
professionally, and value different perspectives. A respectful workplace helps
everyone do their best work.

Harassment and Discrimination
-----------------------------
Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. This includes
behaviour based on personal characteristics or background. Everyone is entitled to
a workplace free from intimidation and unfair treatment.

Confidential Information
------------------------
Protect confidential information belonging to the company, colleagues, and
partners. Share such information only with people who are authorised to have it.
Confidentiality applies both during and after employment.

Conflicts of Interest
---------------------
Disclose conflicts of interest promptly. A conflict of interest is any situation
where personal interests could improperly influence work decisions. Disclosing
early allows the situation to be managed openly and fairly.

Raising Concerns
----------------
Report serious concerns through the internal escalation route. Employees who
raise genuine concerns in good faith should be able to do so without fear of
retaliation. If unsure how to proceed, employees can contact HR for guidance.

Summary
-------
- Treat colleagues respectfully.
- No harassment or discrimination.
- Protect confidential information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Report serious concerns through the internal escalation route.
